# Changelog

User-visible changes to mehla.in. Newest first.
Each entry is dated by the day it went live on `master`. Changes waiting to
ship sit under **Unreleased**.

## Unreleased

_Nothing yet._

## 2026-09-30 (later)

### Added
- A terminal: press `` ` `` (or click `>_` on the homepage) to browse posts,
  projects and links by typing commands (`ls`, `cd`, `cat`, `open`, `neofetch`…), with Tab
  completion. There may be a few hidden ones.

## 2026-09-30

### Changed
- Site rebuilt on Jekyll. Pages render without JavaScript, have proper titles
  and link previews, and posts return a real 200 instead of the 404 redirect.
- Homepage refreshed: updated role, current and side projects described
  by idea only (no names, domains or workflows), and the tagline
  "Software, systems, and what comes next".
- Post titles use typographic quotes.
- No more flash of the light theme for dark-mode visitors.

### Added
- Post: "My Agents Never Said 'I Don't Know'".
- RSS feed at `/feed.xml` and sitemap at `/sitemap.xml`.
- Proper 404 page.

### Removed
- Client-side router, `marked.js`, `posts.json` and the local Python server.
